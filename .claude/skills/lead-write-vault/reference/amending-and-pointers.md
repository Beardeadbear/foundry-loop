# Amending and pointers

> Use when: you must change a ruling, decision or approved plan, or a rule needs to refer to "the current plan".

## Amending a settled document
A ruling, decision or approved plan is amended in place with a dated tag, never rewritten silently:
`> **AMENDED YYYY-MM-DD (operator-directed):** <what changed>. <why>. <what it costs>.` The lead does not redesign
an approved spec; a change goes to the operator.

## The active epic is a pointer
When a rule needs "the plan", cite `active_epic` in `CLAUDE.md`, never a plan's filename. Epics end; rules do
not. With no active epic, `ready` stops being inheritable: re-triage every ready card before dispatch.
